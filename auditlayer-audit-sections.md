# AuditLayer Full Audit — Additional Sections

Five sections to add to the 20-section audit, per Narin:

### 1. Thumbnail Strategy
- Why thumbnails matter for discovery (first thing people see before the caption)
- Thumbnail ideas specific to their content type and pillars
- Visual consistency: fonts, colors, face vs. no face, text overlay style
- A/B testing thumbnails vs. current approach

### 2. Platform Exclusivity
- Leveraging Instagram to share content available nowhere else
- Articles, insights, behind-the-scenes that don't live on their website or other platforms
- Creates a reason to follow specifically on IG
- "You can only get this here" energy

### 3. Collaboration Targets
- Three specific accounts the account holder should reach out to for collaborations
- Why each one fits their niche
- Suggested collab format (joint Live, Reel trade, carousel feature, etc.)
- Outreach template or approach

### 4. Highlights Optimization
- Importance of a clean, intentional Highlights section
- What visitors learn from Highlights in 5 seconds
- Suggested categories and cover designs
- Removing stale, off-brand, or empty Highlights

### 5. Strategic Following
- Adding new, relevant follows monthly as a growth lever
- Who to follow: complementary accounts, potential collaborators, engaged community members
- The algorithm reward: active following signals platform engagement
- Monthly following audit — unfollow inactive/irrelevant accounts
