### 3. Brand Voice: The Nima Vibe

@atllasai will adopt the same raw, documentary-style founder energy that @neema_bardi has built across Instagram (10K) and TikTok (8.8K). This is not a corporate brand account. It's a startup run by a guy in his 20s, building in public, and the content should feel like it.

**What we're channeling:**

- **Documentary, not commercial.** "Filming our journey until we hit $1M/month." Raw, unpolished, real.
- **Building in public.** Late nights, product updates, customer wins, struggles — all of it.
- **Fast-paced editing.** CapCut style — quick cuts, text overlays, trending audio, meme energy.
- **Vulnerability.** Not just wins. The hard parts too. "The long way is the shortcut."
- **Voiceover narration.** Nima's voice clone or real voice over footage. First-person storytelling.
- **Reaction content.** Customer DMs, competitor claims, industry news — reacted to, not just reported.
- **Fitness/lifestyle woven in.** Startup grind + personal life. The whole founder, not just the CEO.
- **Real people, not just product.** The team behind the code — engineers, sales, support. Faces, names, personalities. Who's building this thing.

**The content ecosystem:**

| Role | Screen Time | Vibe |
|---|---|---|
| **Nima (CEO)** | 60% — main face | Founder energy. Voice clone demos. Building in public. The guy. |
| **Team** | 25% — supporting cast | Engineers shipping late. Sales closing deals. Support handling calls. Real humans. |
| **Agents (customers)** | 15% — proof | Testimonials. Transformations. Before/after. They're the heroes. |

**Weekly on-site shoot:** Narin will be on-site at Atllas HQ once per week to capture raw content — Nima's day, team moments, office energy, product development, customer calls. This is the content factory. Everything else is edited from this footage.

**The rule:** If it feels like a polished SaaS ad, kill it. If it feels like something Nima would post on @neema_bardi, ship it. If it doesn't show a real human face, ask why not.

### 4. Content Mix & Storytelling Commitment

Every piece of content runs through: **hero (the agent) → villain (missed calls, dead leads) → transformation (AI books, agent closes).** If it doesn't have all three, it doesn't ship.

| Pillar | % | Storytelling Angle | Weekly Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Product in Action** | 35% | Not "here's a feature." It's "watch this agent sleep through 30 calls and wake up to 5 booked appointments." AI is the sidekick — the agent is the hero. Shot like a documentary, not a demo. | 1-2 Reels |
| **Agent Transformations** | 25% | Before/after: "I used to do 200 dials/day. Now my AI qualifies leads while I'm at my kid's soccer game." Real agents, real numbers, real faces. Reaction-style format. | 1 Reel |
| **Industry Narrative** | 25% | "Cold calling is dead. Here's the data." Positioning Atllas as inevitable. Meme format, green-screen, or voiceover. Not a slide deck. | 1 Carousel or Reel |
| **Founder / Building in Public** | 15% | Nima as the face. Voice clone demos. Late nights. Team cameos — engineers shipping, sales closing, real moments from the weekly shoot. "This is who's building your AI." Raw, personal, human. | 1 Reel |

**Daily Stories (5–7/week):** "Call of the day" breakdowns, behind-the-scenes, polls, agent DMs, competitor reactions, Nima's day.

#### Weekly Content Calendar

| Day | Format | Story Arc |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Reel | **Agent transformation** — before/after arc. Real agent, real numbers. Reaction-style. |
| **Tue** | Carousel or Reel | **Industry narrative** — "3 stats that prove cold calling is dead." Meme or voiceover format. |
| **Wed** | Reel | **Product in action** — AI call demo. Short, punchy, documentary-style. |
| **Thu** | Reel | **Founder BTS or team spotlight** — Nima building, voice clone dev, engineer shipping late, sales team celebrating a close. Real people, real moments from the weekly shoot. |
| **Fri** | Reel | **Product in action or agent story** — second demo or second transformation. |
| **Sat** | Stories | "Call of the week" breakdown. Polls. Real AI call snippet. |
| **Sun** | Stories | Industry reaction. Competitor news. "What we're watching." |
