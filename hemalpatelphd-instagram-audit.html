Content Format Arsenal

1

Carousel "Save Bait" — 2×/week

Instagram carousels get 2–3x the reach of single images because users swipe through and dwell longer. Template: Slide 1 = bold claim hook → Slides 2–6 = one fact per slide with simple graphic → Slide 7 = "Save this for later · Share with someone."

10 ready ideas: "5 signs your mitochondria are struggling" · "What 10 minutes of meditation does to your cells" · "Cold vs. heat: which is better for mitochondria?" · "The NASA twin study and cellular aging" · "E-cigarettes and your cells — what the data actually shows" · "How we measure mitochondrial function from a drop of blood" · "The caveolae-mitochondria connection in 5 slides" · "What 'cellular resilience' actually means" · "Why your mitochondria care about your cell membranes" · "Meditation vs. placebo — what our Nature paper found"

2

60-Second Reels — 3×/week

Reels are Instagram's #1 growth lever, reaching non-followers at 3–5x the rate of other formats. Three formats to rotate:

A — "One Thing" Explainer: Hook in first 2s → explain one concept with B-roll → "Follow for more · Comment your question."
B — Paper Breakdown: Hook with finding → 3 key findings as text overlay → "Full paper in bio · Save for journal club."
C — Behind the Lab: Raw BTS clip (pipetting, microscope, liquid nitrogen) → narration explaining → "AMA below."

Critical rules: text on screen for the entire video (85% watch without sound), hook in first 1.5 seconds (no intro, no logo), 3–5 niche-specific hashtags max.

3

Story Engagement Loops — Daily

Stories keep the algorithm warm between posts and drive DMs, which Instagram weights heavily. Daily cadence: Morning BTS photo + poll · Midday share an old carousel with "Still relevant?" sticker · Evening Q&A box ("Ask me anything about mitochondria"). Reply to every story reply — DMs from stories are a massive algorithmic signal.

4

Community Flywheel — Every Post

In captions: ask a specific question (not "comment below"). ✅ "What's one thing you do for cellular health that most people don't?" ❌ "Comment your thoughts!" · Reply to the first 20 comments within the first hour (triggers notification loops). · Use Instagram's Collab feature with @discovermescreen and podcast hosts to reach both audiences simultaneously.

5

80/20 Value-to-Promotion Ratio

Current mix: ~50% product posts. Target: only 1 in every 5 posts mentions mescreen™. When he does, frame it as the logical next step after the value he just provided, not a standalone ad. The content builds trust; the product becomes the obvious conclusion.

Podcast → Instagram Funnel Strategy

Podcast appearances are the account's highest-leverage growth channel — a new audience hears him speak for 30–90 minutes, trusts him by the end, and can be converted into Instagram followers. But currently, that conversion isn't happening. Here's how to build the flywheel.

The Flywheel

Podcast appearance → listeners trust him → find him on Instagram → see high-quality content → follow → share → more podcast hosts discover him → repeat

Pre-Podcast Checklist (Send to Host Before Recording)

1

Ask the host to plug your Instagram specifically

Most hosts say "find Dr. Patel online." That's wasted. Ask for: "Follow Dr. Patel on Instagram at @hemalpatelphd — he posts breakdowns of mitochondrial research you won't find anywhere else." A specific reason to follow converts 3–5x better than a generic mention.

2

Prepare one "Instagram exclusive" teaser

During the podcast, mention something you'll post on Instagram next week — a visual breakdown of a study you discussed, a behind-the-scenes lab clip, or a carousel expanding on a point you made. Say it on air: "I'm actually posting a 7-slide breakdown of that study on my Instagram next week." This creates FOMO-driven follows.

3

Offer a free resource (lead magnet)

Mention a free download available via your bio link: "5 Signs Your Mitochondria Are Struggling" PDF, "The Mitochondrial Health Starter Guide," or a meditation protocol tied to cellular health. Podcast listeners who download something are the most likely to follow and become loyal community members.

Post-Podcast Content Repurposing (The 72-Hour Sprint)

TimingActionFormat
Day of release Clip the most compelling 60 seconds into a Reel with captions. Hook: the strongest claim or most surprising fact from the episode. Reel + Story
Day 1 Turn 3 talking points from the episode into a carousel. Slide 1 = provocative question from the podcast. Slides 2–7 = your answers, simplified. Carousel
Day 2 Post a "1 thing I wish I'd said" Reel — pick one topic you felt you didn't fully explain and expand on it. Reel

🔁 One podcast = one week of Instagram content. A single 60-minute episode contains roughly 15–20 distinct talking points. Each one can become a carousel slide, a Reel, or a Story. This is not extra work — it's format translation of content you've already created.

Building the Flywheel — Getting More Podcast Invites

  1. Tag the host in every repurposed post. They see free promotion and are more likely to recommend you to other hosts.
  2. Share audience metrics with future hosts. "My last podcast appearance drove 87 new Instagram followers and 200+ link clicks." Hosts book guests who bring audiences.
  3. Pitch adjacent shows. After appearing on a biohacking podcast, pitch longevity, meditation, and academic-science shows. The Instagram content from the first appearance becomes your portfolio.
  4. Use the Collab feature. When you post podcast clips, use Instagram's Collab post with the host's account. Both audiences see it. This is free cross-promotion.

Real fluorescence microscopy images, cell cultures, pipetting BTS, liquid nitrogen vapor. His actual lab produces stunning visuals. Use them as backgrounds for text overlays. Nothing says "real scientist" like real science imagery.

Avoid This

Stock Wellness Photography

No stock photos of people meditating on mountains. No generic DNA helix graphics. No yoga silhouettes at sunset. These signal "wellness influencer" — exactly the category he should distinguish himself from.

Do This

Data Visualizations as Design

His papers contain graphs, charts, and diagrams. Pull these directly into carousels and Reels — simplified, with his voice-over explaining them. Data is his moat. Very few accounts can show actual research data. He can.

Avoid This

Clip Art & Generic Icons

No cartoon mitochondria. No emoji-heavy slides. No icon packs from Canva. Every visual element should either be from his actual work (lab photos, data, paper figures) or be so minimal it disappears.

Template System — 3 Graphic Formats to Standardize

1

Carousel Title Slide

Dark teal background. White Inter Bold. One provocative sentence. Small "Dr. Hemal Patel · UCSD" lockup in the bottom corner. Consistent every time — followers recognize it in the feed instantly.

2

Carousel Content Slide

Off-white background with a microscopy image at 30% opacity behind text. Dark text, one fact per slide. Amber highlight for key numbers. No more than 20 words per slide.

3

Reel Thumbnail

Teal gradient overlay on a frame from the video. White bold text: the hook. His face visible somewhere in the frame. Consistent thumbnail treatment signals a professional account to the algorithm and to new visitors.

🎨 Execution note: These templates can be built once in Canva or Figma and reused indefinitely. A designer can create the master files in an afternoon. After that, producing a new carousel is a 15-minute content-fill exercise, not a design project.

Feed Aesthetic Strategy

Don't overthink the grid. The current best practice for educational accounts is a dark-light alternating row pattern: one row of two dark-background posts (carousel title slides, Reel thumbnails), next row of two light-background posts (content slides, lab photos). This creates visual rhythm without requiring every post to match a rigid color scheme. It also means the template system above naturally produces a cohesive feed.
